1.3.6 problem 34

There are (525) equally likely 5-card hands.

(a) Choose a suit in (41) ways, then choose 5 of its 13 ranks in (135) ways. This includes the royal flush for that suit, so we subtract the 1 way to get the royal flush. Hence, the probability of a flush (excluding the royal flush) is

(41)[(135)−1](525)

(b) Choose the 2 ranks that form the pairs in (132) ways, then choose 2 suits out of 4 for each of those ranks in (42)2 ways. Then choose the rank of the remaining card from the 11 leftover ranks in (111) ways, and its suit in (41) ways. Hence, the probability of two pair is

(132)(42)2(111)(41)(525)
